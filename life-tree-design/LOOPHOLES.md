## 1. THE MASTER PRINCIPLE — stage-gated manifestation

**The tree's life stage is the master clock.** The tree has its locked
chronological stages (seed → seedling → sapling → pole → mature →
old-growth); every stage has a CAPACITY (how many leaves, branches,
flowers, and structures the tree can honestly express); and every
visual manifestation passes through the stage gate. Data is never
lost and never unrewarded — it is BANKED in stage-appropriate form
(buds, clusters, pending structures) until the tree can express it.
Botany first; gamification second; the two never contradict.

**The three banking forms:**
- **Buds** — earned-but-not-yet-expressible achievements/events
  (visible, claimed, explainable — "blooms when the tree reaches its
  flowering stage")
- **Clusters** — aggregated content at early stages (a twig's worth
  of entries = one leaf cluster; per-entry granularity unlocks with
  stage)
- **Branch-buds** — sections present from day one as buds on the
  trunk; the branch extends when sustained presence earns its first
  twigs

**The first-flowering event:** at the flowering stage, all banked
achievement buds burst into the first bloom together — the earned
cherry-blossom moment.

**The master clock (LOCKED - D090):** one STAGE clock, derived from
growth across ANY domain. Tick rules: SEED->SEEDLING = first logged
event - SEEDLING->SAPLING = first sustained presence period (>=15 in-window days in any 30-day window, any-domain-mixed - D115; the window completes INCLUSIVELY on the day the 15th in-window day lands - the paper-run pin, now in the register B2) - SAPLING->POLE = first qualifying year
(any-domain, anchored) - POLE->MATURE = DERIVED MATURITY (structural
threshold, pioneer-speed) - MATURE->OLD-GROWTH = decade scale. One
frozen birth anchor (the first in-window event; never shifts;
existence is monotonic once born - D114). Rings = a BRAND
(decoupled from the clock; the SIX CORE domains - the D116 ring fold:
  journal, habits, gym, nutrition, body, media - calendar-neutral, never chopped). Calendar seasons =
visual-only.

### L-02 Early achievements are flowers, but trees flower at maturity
(RESOLVED): pre-maturity achievements = flower BUDS; the first
flowering stage bursts the Sprout→Heartwood bank into the first
bloom together (the earned cherry-blossom moment); Ring/Grove follow
the tier schedule (Resolution #2). Post-maturity, achievements bloom
directly. Botany + gamification both satisfied; nothing unrewarded.

### L-03 Branches don't exist on a seedling (RESOLVED): the 5
sections exist from the SEEDLING stage as BRANCH-BUDS on the stem
(the seedling's architecture is set, growth varies — real botany;
the seed itself is the closed package — coat + embryo + food). The
branch extends when sustained presence earns its first twigs; the
first branch is a visible milestone.

### L-04 Leaf-per-entry overflows early growth (RESOLVED): leaf
production scales with the tree's surface. Early stages aggregate
entries into LEAF CLUSTERS per twig; per-entry granularity unlocks at
later stages (also a zoom mechanic). A seedling shows a handful of
leaves, never 50.

### L-05 Rare achievements can fire early but map to old-tree states
(RESOLVED): the achievement is earned + claimed immediately (trophy
system untouched); its tree-visual is BANKED as a rare bud and
manifests per the tier schedule (Resolution #2 — Grove manifests at
the next annual bloom post-maturity). The D086 flower-layer fallback
becomes stage-aware. Nothing unrewarded, tree never incoherent.
