## 1. The rarity spine — the six-tier ladder (the ONLY tier system)

From the locked achievement docs (v2:8-24), verbatim definitions:

| Tier | Definition | Tree flower magnitude (D086 mapping) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sprout** | a first: the first time you did the thing at all | the smallest bloom — a single common flower |
| **Root** | the thing took hold: short but real consistency (weeks, not days) | a modest flower cluster |
| **Branch** | the thing extended somewhere new: a new domain, a new personal best, a new kind of entry | a prominent flower family (inflorescence type) |
| **Heartwood** | the thing became structural: true for months, load-bearing now | a rare flower family + a small visual accent |
| **Ring** | a full year passed and the thing was still true. Literally one ring per year, like the tree | a distinctive bloom + a branch-level visual event |
| **Grove** | multi-year, decade-scale, or genetically-ceiling-tier. The rare, quiet, huge ones | the LARGE visuals: full floral events, transformations (D086 top tier) |

**Multi-tier trophies** (e.g., Novel-Length Life Root→Branch→Heartwood→Grove)
fire SEPARATELY at each tier as their thresholds cross — **each
tier-step is its own rarity event** and its own flower.

**Coach loudness by tier (locked):** ONLY Ring and Grove receive Coach
appreciation (one sincere derived line); all other tiers are a silent
in-game toast. One Coach line AT MOST per trophy fire.

## 4. THE THREE HARD CLUSTERS - the LARGE visuals (D086 top tier)

The docs name THREE clusters as the genuinely hardest - these are the
candidates for the top-of-ladder LARGE unique visuals (the
deterministic-core + derived-accents transformation events):

1. **The robot-consistency family** (strictest, least forgiving):
   Same Time Every Time (I-4, 60d +/-30min) - Like Clockwork (II-9,
   90 completions) - The Schedule Never Breaks (III-22, 26wk exact
   weekday-set) - Same Hour Same Scale (V-3, 26wk weekday+slot) - No
   Deviation (IV-5, 30 consecutive +/-3% logged days) - capstone:
   **Ghost in the Machine (IX-5, 90-day three-way overlap)**. NO
   GRACE; an unlogged day is a HARD MISS; only user-declared planned
   rest freezes a run.
2. **The Vow/Old Growth cluster** ("deliberately the rarest things in
   the whole system"): The Three-Year Vow (VIII-7), The Five-Year Vow
   (VIII-8), Old Growth (VIII-9, 10 consecutive six-domain years),
   Ouroboros (VIII-10, 10 consecutive years, live-streak restart
   semantics). Old Growth = "the ceiling achievement of the entire
   system". The Five-Year Vow guardrail: "the hardest achievement in
   the system to fake".
3. **Ghost in the Machine (IX-5)** - Grove capstone: three already-
   guarded, independently-real achievements overlapping on the same
   90 real days. "The odds of that happening by accident are
   effectively zero, which is exactly the intent."

Secondary difficulty axes (each feeds the ladder's upper placement):
multi-year chains (x3/x5 yearly bars - no summing, no skipped year),
no-miss week/month ladders, one-shot window/slot strictness, strict-
streak-only rules, ceiling/decade-scale (Yew 10 rings, Ten Years),
genetic ceilings (The Estimated Ceiling, Dragon Slayer 260kg DL, The
Monolith 220kg squat, The Furnace 140kg bench, Atlas Press 100kg OHP,
The Brand 5,000,000kg lifetime tonnage), cross-domain coincidence
(Wrote It Down, Eyes on the Data, Six for Six, The Living Archive -
pairing two hard-to-fake things).

## 5. GAMIFICATION CONSTRAINTS THE TREE MUST RESPECT (from the scan)

- Derived-only (non-negotiable): every achievement/streak/ring is
  COMPUTED from the event log via H3 owner functions - never stored
  counters, never user-editable, never buyable. The tree consumes the
  SAME owner outputs.
- Forbidden (never rewards): opening the app, browsing screens,
  empty/blank saves, retroactive/bulk logging, restoring old streaks,
  logging itself, imported content, **trophies/achievements (ZERO
  XP)**, cosmetic actions, settings edits.
- Grace is 1 day per 7-day window (the "2-day rule" is research
  heritage only - NOT in the system).
- Two word floors: >=20 (journal XP gate) vs >=40 (qualifying-entry
  floor). Imported entries never count.
- No claim/progress tables exist: gamification is fully derived from
  events (XP, streaks, achievements).
- Gold is streaks-only (design rule) - the tree must not use gold for
  achievements.
