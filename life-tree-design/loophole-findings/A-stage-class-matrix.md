## B. THE STAGE MODEL ITSELF (audit point 3)

- **M-01 · MAJOR · Stage model.** The six-stage spine (seed → seedling → sapling →
  pole → mature → old-growth) matches MASTER P11.2's chronology in ORDER. But the
  design's durations are compressed ~10–30× vs real botany (seedling = P3.1's "first
  1–5 years" → weeks–months; sapling = years 2–10+ → "~year 1–2"). Compression for
  UX is a defensible gamification choice — it must be recorded as such in the engine
  contract, not silently assumed. Also: germination (P2) and death (P11.2 stage 7)
  are folded away — fine for an app tree; recommend at least a **germination beat**
  (the seed's coat crack, P2.1 radicle emergence) at the SEED→SEEDLING transition so
  the seedling's arrival is a visible milestone, matching the user's seed-planting
  moment. Severity: MAJOR only because the compression is currently undocumented and
  the boundary rule (what exactly flips a user from SAPLING to POLE) is unspecified.
- **M-02 · MINOR · Stage boundaries.** SAPLING "~year 1–2" and POLE "years 2–4"
  overlap at year 2. The engine contract must define the transition trigger exactly
  (candidate: qualifying-year count, not calendar years — a user's tree shouldn't
  pole at year 2 of near-zero logging). Derivation from qualifying years is the
  existing currency (D085 ring rule); state it explicitly.
- **M-03 · MINOR · Stage names vs botany.** "POLE" is a real forester term (P11.2
  stage 4 — canopy rise, self-pruning) — correct usage, keep it. But the design's
  MATURE stage ("years 4+") means "big enough to bloom" while botany's mature means
  "flowering begins" — the names align once C-01 is fixed. No action beyond C-01.

## C. THE CAPACITY TABLE (§2) — audit point "capacities" + point 1/2

- **C-05** — OLD-GROWTH leaf capacity "—" (see Critical list).
- **M-04 · MAJOR · SEED row: "0 (5 branch-buds on the trunk)".** A seed has NO trunk —
  the trunk (primary stem from the plumule) exists only after germination (P1.2, P2.1,
  P11.2). L-03's locked text ("5 branch-buds on the trunk from day one") inherits the
  same impossibility: on day one the tree IS the seed. Fix: the seed is a bare seed;
  the **5 branch-buds appear on the first stem at germination/seedling** (botanically
  = axillary buds on the seedling shoot, P4.3 — which is exactly where L-03's intent
  "the seedling's architecture is set, growth varies" actually lives). Also the cell
  is self-notational: capacity 0 + 5 buds listed is fine per the banking convention,
  but only if the buds are drawn on the stem, not the seed.
- **M-05 · MAJOR · SEEDLING row: "first branch extends on earned presence".** Botany:
  a seedling has NO side branches — apical dominance suppresses every axillary bud
  (P4.5: the terminal bud's auxin; P11.2: sapling = "strong apical dominance, one
  straight leader"; P3: a first-year oak = "one 20 cm shoot"). The first branch
  cannot extend in the weeks–months seedling window. Fix: first branch extension is a
  **SAPLING** event (branching begins when the leader is established — P3.4's
  transition); the SEEDLING row keeps 5 branch-buds (banked) + swelling with presence.
- **M-06 · MAJOR · SAPLING row: "FIRST BLOOM".** See C-01 (this is the capacity
  table's copy of the same error).
- **M-07 · MAJOR · MATURE row: "leader expressed".** The leader is the SAPLING
  character, not the mature one. P11.2: the sapling has "strong apical dominance, one
  straight leader"; by MATURE the height plateaus, the crown spreads and the leader's
  dominance is spent (P4.5: decurrent crowns lose the leader). Fix: SAPLING =
  "leader expressed" (the most sustained domain is visibly the crown's boss — D088);
  MATURE = "full crown; leader's reign history" (the leader is now the highest, not
  the only, axis).
- **M-08 · MAJOR · OLD-GROWTH row: "modifications + rare structural layer".**
  Contradicts the LOCKED D089 split: rare STRUCTURAL modifications (caudex, buttress,
  phyllodes, cladode, thorns, storage leaves) have a floor of **2+ qualifying years**
  — i.e., POLE/MATURE — and SUBTLE details (reaction wood, epicormic, bracts, bud
  scales, …) have **no tenure gate at all**. Gating every modification to the
  decade+ stage both delays D089's floors and misrepresents the adaptation layer as a
  stage capacity. Fix: the capacity table's OLD-GROWTH row should read "full
  structural layer (D089 floors already met) + the largest visuals"; the adaptation
  gates live in D089 (achievement-triggered, tenure-floored), NOT in the stage table.
- **M-09 · MAJOR · The twig feed vs the stage gate (temporal-fidelity).** D088
  (LOCKED): "one twig per month of sustained presence per domain" — a feed that fires
  from month 1. The matrix gates twigs at SAPLING (~year 1–2). Months 1–12 of a
  consistent user therefore produce ~12–60 earned-but-unexpressible twigs with no
  banking form named. This is the exact early-fire/late-expression loophole class
  §3 exists to catch. Fix: bank the first year's twig-earnings as **branch-bud
  swelling** (visible, explainable — "your journal branch has earned 4 twigs; it
  becomes a branch when the tree reaches its sapling stage") or start the monthly
  twig feed at the sapling transition — one of the two, decided at the input-map step.
- **M-10 · MINOR · "Capacities are exact numbers" (table note).** The table claims
  exact engine numbers but every value except 0/5 is qualitative ("full", "begins").
  Acknowledged as a design draft — but flag for Step 8: the contract needs real
  numbers per stage per organ (e.g., seedling = 1–3 leaf clusters max — P3: a
  seedling's first year is "one 20 cm shoot", NOT 50 leaves — L-04's "handful").

**Capacity progression verdict:** branch capacity 0→5-buds→extend→full is the right
shape (with M-04/M-05 placement fixes). Leaf clusters→granularity is the right zoom
mechanics (L-04) and is botanically supported by heteroblasty (P3.2: juveniles bear
different, fewer leaves than adults). Flower capacity 0→full is coherent once the
bloom stage is moved (C-01). The progression concept is sound; the placements are not.

## E. CROSS-CELL CONTRADICTION REGISTER (audit point 5)

1. **Reproductive axis vs botany + itself:** C-01/C-02/C-03/M-22/M-26 — the fruit
   column (class 5) and flower column (class 6) contradict each other's stage clocks
   (fruit buds at SEEDLING while flowers are buds-only; fruits in the same stage as

   SEEDLING; class 1 SP + the capacity table put twigs at SAPLING; D088's monthly
   twig feed (M-09) fires from month 1. Three different twig clocks in one design.
   The input-map step must pick ONE: twigs begin at SAPLING, with months 1–12 banked
   (M-09's branch-bud swelling).
3. **Modification gates:** M-08/M-12/M-23/M-31 — the matrix's stage columns gate
   modifications (storage leaves, tendrils, adaptations) by stage; D088/D089 (LOCKED)
   gate them by achievement-trigger + tenure floor. Draft loses to locked — reconcile
   the table to D089.
4. **Seasonality:** M-21/M-29 — the matrix defers seasonal rhythm and dormancy to
   POLE/SAPLING; D085 (LOCKED) starts the calendar skeleton at year one. D085 wins.
5. **Leader:** M-07 — capacity table expresses the leader at MATURE; botany's apical
   dominance is the sapling's character. Fix the placement.
