"minimal-but-perfect," and phyllodes stops being rare.** A daily weigher
reads 0.05 — identical to a daily journaler (run 02) and a 3x/week lifter

STUBBORN" is the wrong label for 365/365 perfection; the signature reads
volume, not gaps. *Tuning:* (a) calibrate the ceiling via the dev tools
(D105) — run-02's options (b) lower to ~6–8 events/day or (c) count
entry-CLASSES (1/day = 1/7 ≈ 0.14); AND (b) add a density/gap term to E3
(e.g., resource ≤0.4 AND <26 twigs/yr, or a gap-density bar) so phyllodes
means "survives on little with real lapses," not "one perfect tiny act daily."
Recommendation: both — the axis should tell "a rich life vs a sparse one,"
and phyllodes should stay rare.
